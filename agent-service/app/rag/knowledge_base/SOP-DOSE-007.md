# SOP-DOSE-007: Chemical Dosing Skid Calibration

## Purpose

Keep the biocide and scale-inhibitor dosing skids on Site 12 within their
calibrated delivery bands so treatment chemistry stays effective.

## Normal operating range

Dosing pump stroke rate 12-18 strokes/min, injection pressure 2.0-3.5 bar,
treated-water conductivity 950-1100 uS/cm. A conductivity drop below 900
uS/cm with normal flow usually means under-dosing, not a pump fault.

## Symptoms of dosing drift

- Conductivity drifts below band while flow and temperature stay normal.
- Residual biocide readings fall below the posted minimum at the daily test
  point.
- Dosing tank level falls slower than the calculated consumption curve.

## Response procedure

1. Verify dosing pump stroke rate against the setpoint; recalibrate if the
   deviation exceeds 10%.
2. Inspect injection check valves for scaling or blockage.
3. Confirm tank concentration with a refractometer reading before assuming
   pump failure.
4. Log the calibration result and the operator on shift.

## Escalation

If conductivity stays below band for more than 2 hours after recalibration,
notify the site water-treatment lead; sustained under-dosing risks
microbiological growth in the cooling loop.
