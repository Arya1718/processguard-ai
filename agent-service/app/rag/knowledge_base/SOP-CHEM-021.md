# SOP-CHEM-021: Cooling Tower pH and Conductivity Excursion

## Purpose

Diagnose pH or conductivity excursions in the cooling tower basin when the
water chemistry leaves its normal band without a corresponding flow or
vibration problem.

## Normal operating range

Basin pH 7.5-8.2, conductivity 950-1100 uS/cm. Flow and vibration are
expected to remain in band during a pure chemistry excursion.

## Symptoms of a chemistry excursion

- pH drifts below 7.5 or above 8.2 while flow stays above 118 m3/h.
- Conductivity climbs above 1100 uS/cm over hours, not seconds.
- No change in pump vibration or supply temperature.

## Response procedure

1. Check the acid/caustic dosing setpoints; an incorrect setpoint is the
   most common cause of a slow pH drift.
2. Sample the basin water and compare against the lab result before making
   large setpoint changes.
3. Verify the makeup-water quality; a source switch can shift conductivity
   for an entire shift.
4. Increase blowdown temporarily if conductivity exceeds 1200 uS/cm.

## Escalation

Contact the water-treatment lead if pH stays outside 7.0-8.5 for more than
one hour; corrosion risk rises quickly outside that window.
